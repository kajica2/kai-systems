Voice AI · v1.0

A voice that sounds like you, on a phone, this weekend.

The 5-layer voice-AI stack: listen, think, speak, clone, ship. Each layer is one or two tools, and the whole pipeline ships in a single weekend. No call center. No IVR hell. Just a voice that sounds like you.

01 · The layers

Five jobs. Twelve tools. One voice that ships.

Every voice-AI app is a sequence of five jobs. Listen (turn audio into text), think (run an LLM on the text), speak (turn the answer into audio), clone (make it sound like you), ship (wire it to a phone).

1
Listen · speech-to-text
Whisper is the floor. MLX Whisper is the no-cloud version.

Whisper is fast, multilingual, and runs at the edge via MLX on Apple Silicon. Below 200ms latency for a 5-second utterance, which is fast enough for a phone conversation.

Whisper— OpenAI's STT MLX Whisper— on-device STT
2
Think · the brain
Small, fast, cheap. Latency beats intelligence in real-time.

A voice conversation needs sub-second response time. Claude Haiku and GPT-4o-mini both fit. The smart model runs offline for context, the small model runs the live loop.

Claude Haiku— fast, low-cost GPT-4o-mini— cheap fallback
3
Speak · text-to-speech
ElevenLabs for the polish. OpenAI TTS for the cheap fallback.

ElevenLabs sets the bar in 2026 — natural inflection, emotional range, multilingual. OpenAI TTS is the budget option for non-critical paths (status updates, scheduled readouts).

ElevenLabs— the polish OpenAI TTS— the budget PlayHT— alt voice catalog
4
Clone · your voice
30 seconds of clean audio is all you need.

ElevenLabs Voice Design and Resemble AI both clone from 30 seconds of clean audio. The result is "sounds like you, but more articulate and never tired." Use it sparingly — voice clones that pass 100% of the time also feel uncanny.

ElevenLabs Voice Design— instant clone Resemble AI— pro clone
5
Ship · deploy + channels
From a Python script to a phone number in 20 minutes.

Vapi gives you a phone number + the orchestration layer in one product. LiveKit is the lower-level WebRTC for custom UIs. Twilio is the carrier. Pick the one that matches the build cost you can stomach.

Vapi— phone + orchestration LiveKit— WebRTC low-level Twilio— the carrier
02 · Why

Voice is the next interface. Most voice apps fail.

The 2024-25 wave of "AI call center" products proved you can ship a voice agent. They also proved that 90% of them sound like robots and solve problems nobody has. The win is at the personal scale: a voice that knows you, talks like you, runs on your number.

Signal: "I want a phone number I can hand to clients" · "I want to read my daily brief in my own voice" · "I want a podcast that runs in my voice" · "I want my voicemail to be handled by a thing that knows me"
↓
Action: Build a personal voice agent. Voice clone yourself (30 seconds of audio). Wire ElevenLabs TTS to your number via Vapi. Set the system prompt to your context (calendar, inbox, recent notes). Total build: a weekend.
Signal: "But I don't want to talk to a robot" · "I want this for my team, not just me" · "The latency is bad" · "I tried this in 2024 and the voice was uncanny"

03 · The artifact

The system prompt for a personal voice agent.

The voice agent is just an LLM with a voice attached. The system prompt is where you encode the persona, the context, the guardrails. The template that works.

# Voice agent system prompt · ~400 words · for Claude Haiku 4.5

ROLE
You are [your name]'s personal voice agent. You answer the phone when
[your name] can't. You speak in [your name]'s voice, with [your name]'s
cadence and [your name]'s vocabulary. You are not a generic assistant.

WHO YOU TALK TO
- Clients calling about active projects
- Vendors calling about quotes / invoices / scheduling
- Friends calling to make plans
- Strangers calling the wrong number (be brief, redirect)

CONTEXT YOU HAVE
- Today's calendar (next 8 hours)
- Last 5 emails sent to this caller (if email matched)
- 3 most recent project notes (if relevant)
- [Your name]'s preferences (no calls before 9am, no calls after 8pm,
  Friday afternoons are deep-work blocks)

VOICE RULES
- Short answers. 1-3 sentences max per turn. This is a phone, not email.
- No filler ("Sure!", "Of course!", "Great question!"). Start with the substance.
- If you don't know, say so and offer to take a message.
- Never invent facts about [your name]'s schedule, projects, or opinions.
- If asked something outside your context, take a message.

GUARDRAILS
- Never make commitments on [your name]'s behalf (no scheduling, no
  agreeing to deliverables, no price quotes).
- Never share contact info, addresses, or financial details.
- Never engage with harassment — politely end the call and flag.
- If the caller is in crisis, route to the human immediately.

WHEN TO HAND OFF
- Caller asks for [your name] by name → "They're unavailable right now,
  want me to take a message or have them call back?"
- Caller is emotional / upset → "[Your name] is going to call you back
  personally. When works for you?"
- Caller asks something the context doesn't cover → take a detailed
  message, including name, callback number, and a 1-sentence summary.

TONE
Warm. Direct. Not performative. The way a smart friend would answer
your phone if you were in the shower.
04 · In practice

The worked example: a real build, real tools, real timestamps. Saturday morning to Sunday afternoon, one voice agent on a Vapi number.

Personal voice agent. Answers the phone when I'm unavailable. Cloned voice, calendar context, system prompt above. Vapi number, deployed to a single Twilio DID. Budget: $0 in build cost, $20/mo in runtime.

05 · Forking the stack

Same 5 layers, your 12 tools.

The five jobs are universal. The 12 tools are 2026. Swap tools as the field moves; the layer model holds.

Mobile-first (iOS / Android)
Drop Vapi + Twilio. Add LiveKit iOS SDK. Add on-device Whisper via CoreML. The whole loop runs offline once the model is downloaded.
Branded customer support
Add a knowledge base (RAG over your docs). Add function calls for ticket creation, account lookups, refund requests. Drop voice clone — use a polished brand voice.
Podcast / content
Drop the listen + think layers entirely. Use ElevenLabs Long-Form for the entire episode. Add a script prompt for topic, tone, length. Generate 1 hour for ~$5.
Multilingual
ElevenLabs multilingual voices handle EN/ES/FR/DE/IT/PL out of the box. Add per-language system prompts. Whisper large-v3 handles 99 languages for the input.
